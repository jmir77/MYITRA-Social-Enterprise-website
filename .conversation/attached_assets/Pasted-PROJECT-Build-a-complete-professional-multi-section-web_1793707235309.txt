PROJECT
Build a complete, professional, multi-section website for "MYITRA Social Enterprise", a social enterprise operating under INTI International College Subang, Malaysia. Tagline: "Empowering Communities. Inspiring Futures." Secondary line: "People · Purpose · Positive Change". Its flagship project is "Kotak Cantik" (a hygiene box for underserved women and teenage girls), which is currently a Top 20 team in the USEC competition.

AUDIENCE
Corporate CSR teams and sponsors, government agencies, NGOs, competition judges, INTI students and volunteers, and local community members. The site must feel credible, dignified, warm and hopeful. It is NOT a charity-pity site. Show empowerment, dignity and opportunity.

LANGUAGE & LOCALE
English. Malaysia. Currency in RM. Use "B40" as written.

BRAND COLORS (from the logo, a green geometric letter "M")
- Deep green #2D5E27: headings, footer background, dark sections
- Medium green #468B34: primary buttons, links, icons
- Light green #7DD56F: highlights, hover states, accent lines
- Very pale green #EEF8EC: alternating section backgrounds
- Soft pink #F4A6C0 and pale pink #FDEFF4: ONLY for Kotak Cantik sections
- Text dark #1F2A1F, body gray #4A554A, white #FFFFFF
- SDG cards use official SDG colors: SDG1 #E5243B, SDG3 #4C9F38, SDG4 #C5192D, SDG10 #DD1367, SDG17 #19486A

TYPOGRAPHY
Poppins for everything. H1 52px desktop / 34px mobile, bold. H2 38px / 28px, semibold. H3 22px, semibold. Body 17px, line-height 1.7. Use an elegant script font (Dancing Script) ONLY for short quotes and taglines.

LAYOUT & STYLE
Max content width 1200px. Generous white space (section padding 96px desktop / 56px mobile). Alternate section backgrounds between white and very pale green. Cards with 18px rounded corners and soft shadows. Pill-shaped buttons: primary = solid medium green with white text, secondary = green outline. Hover: slight lift and light-green glow. Icons in a consistent line style. Photos with rounded corners. Subtle fade-up on scroll and count-up animation for numbers. No heavy animation, no autoplay audio or video.

HEADER
Sticky white header with shadow on scroll. Left: green M logo + "MYITRA Social Enterprise" with a small line "Under INTI International College Subang". Menu: Home, About, Vision & Mission, Kotak Cantik, Impact, Partners, Get Involved, Contact. Right: button "Partner With Us". Mobile: hamburger menu with the same links and button.

STRUCTURE
Page 1 (one long scrolling Home page with anchor sections, in this order): Hero, Impact Numbers, About Us, Vision, Mission, Objectives, Three Impact Pillars, Kotak Cantik Teaser, SDGs, Partners & Recognition, Venues, Get Involved, Contact. 
Page 2: a separate "Kotak Cantik" page (details below). The menu item "Kotak Cantik" goes to this page.

FOOTER
Deep green background, white text. Columns: logo + one-line description; Quick Links; Contact details; Social icons. Bottom bar: "© 2026 MYITRA Social Enterprise. Under INTI International College Subang." Include the INTI logo.

CONTACT / ENQUIRY FORM
Fields: Full name, Email, Phone, "I am interested in" dropdown (Volunteering, Sponsoring Kotak Cantik, Corporate Partnership, Donating, General Enquiry), Message, a submit button "Send Enquiry", and a thank-you message after submission. Next to the form show email [EMAIL], phone [PHONE], address "Level 9, INTI International College Subang, [FULL ADDRESS]", and social links [INSTAGRAM] [FACEBOOK] [LINKEDIN]. Add a map embed of INTI International College Subang if possible.

SEO & ACCESSIBILITY
One H1 per page. Page titles like "MYITRA Social Enterprise | Empowering Communities. Inspiring Futures." Meta descriptions on every page. Descriptive alt text on every image. Good color contrast, readable font sizes, keyboard-friendly navigation, fast loading, fully mobile responsive.

RULES
Use ONLY the content I provide. Do not invent statistics, testimonials, names, awards or partners. Do not show any personal details of beneficiaries. Do not expand the abbreviation "USEC". Where I have not supplied a photo, insert a neat placeholder block labelled with the photo it needs (e.g. "Photo: women holding Kotak Cantik boxes"). Never use generic stock photos of people.

=== KOTAK CANTIK PAGE ===
Hero (pink and white): Kotak Cantik logo, H1 "Kotak Cantik", tagline "Dignity. Hygiene. Empowerment.", sub-line "More Than a Box. A Box of Dignity, Opportunity and Hope." USEC banner: "Proud to be a Top 20 team in USEC. Finals in Putrajaya, November 2026."

Section "Why Kotak Cantik exists": quote "When survival becomes a priority, personal hygiene becomes a luxury." Text: "In July 2022, a community needs assessment of 780 women from PPR Lembah Subang 1 and 2 asked: 'What matters most to you and your family?' 84% prioritised food, because feeding their families comes first. Only 2% prioritised access to quality personal hygiene products. It does not mean hygiene is unimportant. It means that when money is limited, survival comes first. At MYITRA, we believe no woman should have to choose between feeding her family and protecting her own health, hygiene and dignity." Show 84% and 2% as two big stat cards.

Section "What's inside one box": icon grid with quantities: 10 Sanitary Pads | 10 Panty Liners | 5 Bamboo Toothbrushes | 1 Toothpaste | 1 Bar Soap | 1 Feminine Wash. Photo placeholder: "Photo: the actual Kotak Cantik box with contents".

Section "How it works": 3 steps: 1 "Affordable for the community" – sold at RM22 per box. 2 "Women earn income" – trained women entrepreneurs sell each box and earn a 10% commission (RM2.20). 3 "Sponsors and donors make it reach more" – sponsorships and donations cover the rest so more boxes reach those who need them most. Quote: "When you support a box, you empower a family."

Section "From beneficiaries to social entrepreneurs": "Our long-term vision is to transform women from aid recipients into active economic contributors. Through training in entrepreneurship, financial literacy, digital marketing, personal branding, customer engagement and business management, women build confidence, earn supplementary income and move toward financial independence. Revenue from sales and partnerships is reinvested into more training and more boxes." Quote: "We do not measure success solely by how many people we help today, but by how many people we empower to stand independently tomorrow."

Section "Our Journey" (vertical timeline):
- July 2018: Kotak Cantik launched by INTI International College Subang students as a CSR initiative. About 550 boxes distributed at PPR Lembah Subang 1 with MyRelo.
- November 2018: About 750 boxes distributed at PPR Lembah Subang 1, inaugurated by former KPKT Minister YB Zuraida Kamarudin, with MyRelo and the Ministry of Housing and Local Government.
- 2022: Community needs assessment of 780 women.
- 2024 to 2026: Women entrepreneurship training grows from 5 to 12 women at PPR Lembah Subang 2. With INTI student volunteers, the first 1,500 Kotak Cantik boxes are packed.
- July 2025: SE TUNAS Accreditation from INSKEN/KUSKOP (renewed for 2026).
- 13 June 2026: Community Entrepreneurship & Empowerment Centre launched at PPR Lembah Subang 2.
- November 2026: USEC finals in Putrajaya.
